# EVOLUTION.md — self-evolution doctrine

Rules here evolve by discipline: a practice that fires twice in the
engagement record graduates into this repository's standing rules
(AGENTS.md); a rule that never fires gets pruned at
review. Every rule change names its trigger incident.

Seed: the standing rules were distilled from the 2026-09-28 category survey
([CATEGORY.md](CATEGORY.md)) and the three-wave adversarial build swarm;
each rule carries its own trigger.
